FAQ

We got answers.

Everything you need to know about Studio View.

Is Studio View really free?+
Yes. Creating an account is completely free for both studios and artists. A small session fee of $6 to $20 applies per booking. Paid memberships are optional and give you extra features like booking credits and featured placement.
What is a session fee?+
A session fee is a small charge that applies when a booking is confirmed and paid through the app. It ranges from $6 to $20 depending on the session. This is how Studio View stays sustainable without charging monthly fees upfront.
Do I need a credit card to sign up?+
No credit card required to create your account. You only need payment info when you book a session or upgrade to a paid membership.
What cities is Studio View available in?+
Studio View is live statewide across four launch markets. In New Jersey we cover Newark, Jersey City, Trenton, Camden, and Paterson. In Pennsylvania we cover Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Allentown, and Reading. In California we cover Los Angeles, Compton, Long Beach, Oakland, San Diego, Sacramento, and Inglewood. In Arizona we cover Phoenix, Mesa, Tempe, Scottsdale, Tucson, and Glendale. More states and markets are coming soon.
How do membership booking credits work?+
Artist Select includes $10 per month in booking credits. Artist Gold includes $30 per month. These credits apply automatically at checkout. Unused credits roll over up to $2 on Select and up to $10 on Gold per cycle.
Can I cancel my membership?+
Yes. You can cancel anytime through the app. Your account returns to the Free plan at the end of your billing cycle. No cancellation fees.
How do studios get paid?+
Payments are processed automatically through the app when a session is confirmed. Studios receive their payout minus the session fee. No invoicing, no chasing clients.
What is the Promote Studio feature?+
Studios can boost their listing for sponsored placement in the Browse section. One dollar in promo credit equals one day of boosted visibility. You can load credits and activate boosts from your Studio Owner dashboard.
What is the difference between Artist Select and Artist Gold?+
Both include monthly booking credits and member-only perks. Artist Gold includes $30 per month in credits versus $10 on Select, plus priority booking, weekly discounts, a Gold username, custom profile banner, and featured artist opportunities.
How do I contact support?+
Email us at studioviewapp@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as possible. We are a small team building something real and we take every message seriously.
