How It Works

Simple. Fast.
Built for the culture.

01

Create Your Profile

Studios list their space, rates, and availability. Artists set up in minutes. Both are completely free, no credit card needed.

02

Book or Get Booked

Artists search studios by city, genre, and price. Studios receive booking requests and confirm sessions in real time.

03

Record With Purpose

Pay for your studio time directly in the app. Secure, seamless, automatic. Studios get paid, artists get their time. Stop worrying about the logistics and focus on what matters, making music.

Built For

Two sides.
One platform.

Recording Studios

Stop losing bookings to DMs and spreadsheets. Studio View puts your studio in front of artists actively looking to record right now, for free.

  • Free listing, no monthly fee to get started
  • Manage bookings and availability in one place
  • Automated payments, get paid per session
  • Premium membership for featured placement
  • Build your artist client base organically

Music Artists

Find the right studio for your sound in any city. Book sessions in minutes not days. Focus on the music, we handle everything else.

  • Search studios by city, vibe, and price

  • Session history and receipts in one place
  • Memberships include free studio time
  • Free to sign up and use forever
