How It Works

Simple. Fast.
Built for the culture.

From signing up to your first session, Studio View makes the whole process easy for studios and artists alike.

Step by Step

For Studios

Get your studio in front of artists in minutes.

01

Sign Up Free

Create your Studio View account. No credit card required. Takes less than 2 minutes.

02

Add Your Studio Listing

Add your studio name, hourly rate, gear list, vibe, photos, and availability. The more detail you add, the more bookings you get.

03

Get Discovered by Artists

Your studio appears in search results for artists in your city. Artists browse your profile, check availability, and send booking requests.

04

Confirm Sessions

You receive a booking request with the artist details, session date, and time. Confirm or decline directly from the app.

05

Get Paid Automatically

After the session, payment is processed automatically. You keep your rate, no invoicing, no chasing payments, no hassle.

06

Grow Your Client Base

Repeat bookings, reviews, and featured placement help you build a steady roster of artists. Upgrade to a premium plan to get boosted visibility.

Step by Step

For Artists

Find your studio and book your session in minutes.

01

Sign Up Free

Create your artist profile on Studio View. Free forever. No credit card needed.

02

Search Studios in Your City

Browse studios in Newark, Philadelphia, Los Angeles, and more. Filter by price, genre, and vibe to find the right fit.

03

Check Availability

See real-time availability for each studio. No more back and forth DMs asking if they are open.

04

Send a Booking Request

Request your session date and time. The studio gets notified instantly and confirms or suggests an alternative.

05

Pay Through the App

Once confirmed, pay securely through Studio View. Memberships include monthly booking credits so you can put that money straight toward studio time.

View Membership Plans →
06

Record with Purpose

Show up and focus on the music. Your session details, receipts, and history are all saved in your account.
